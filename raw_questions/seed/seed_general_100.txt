English Language & Literature
21. Choose the correct synonym of "Abandon":
a) Desert
b) Keep
c) Cherish
d) Adopt
Answer: a
Explanation: To abandon is to leave or desert completely.

22. The antonym of "Benevolent" is:
a) Kind
b) Generous
c) Malevolent
d) Charitable
Answer: c
Explanation: Benevolent means well-meaning; malevolent means wishing harm to others.

23. "Hamlet" was written by:
a) Christopher Marlowe
b) William Shakespeare
c) Ben Jonson
d) John Webster
Answer: b
Explanation: Hamlet (c. 1600) is one of Shakespeare's four great tragedies.

24. "Paradise Lost" is an epic by:
a) John Milton
b) Edmund Spenser
c) John Dryden
d) Alexander Pope
Answer: a
Explanation: Milton's blank-verse epic was first published in 1667.

25. Which spelling is correct?
a) Acommodation
b) Accomodation
c) Accommodation
d) Acomodation
Answer: c
Explanation: "Accommodation" has double c and double m.

26. Fill in the blank: He is senior ___ me.
a) than
b) to
c) from
d) over
Answer: b
Explanation: Latin comparatives such as senior, junior, superior and prior take "to", not "than".

27. The passive form of "They are building a bridge" is:
a) A bridge is built by them.
b) A bridge was being built by them.
c) A bridge is being built by them.
d) A bridge has been built by them.
Answer: c
Explanation: Present continuous passive = am/is/are + being + past participle.
